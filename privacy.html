Privacy Notice

Last updated: December 2025 Data Controller: Jack Rigden, Psychotherapist (MBACP)

I take your privacy and confidentiality seriously. This notice explains what personal information I collect, why I collect it, how it is used and stored, and your rights under UK GDPR.

Data Controller contact details jack@mentavera.com 07356 286967 www.mentavera.com

1. What information I collect

I only collect information necessary to provide psychotherapy safely and professionally. This may include:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address
  • Background information: what brings you to therapy
  • Administrative information: invoices, appointment history
  • Clinical information: notes that support ongoing therapeutic work

I do not collect more information than I need.

2. How your information is used

Your data is used for:

  • Providing psychotherapy sessions
  • Arranging appointments and communicating with you
  • Maintaining clinical records in line with professional standards
  • Handling payment, invoicing and administration
  • Meeting legal or ethical obligations (for example, if there is a serious risk of harm)

I do not use your information for marketing or share it with third parties for those purposes.

3. Lawful basis for processing

Under GDPR, the lawful bases I rely on are:

  • Contract: to provide psychotherapy services
  • Legitimate interests: administrative tasks necessary for running the practice
  • Vital interests / Legal obligation: where there is a serious risk of harm or legal requirement
  • Special category data (health information): processed under Article 9(2)(h) — provision of health care

4. How your information is stored

  • Your information is stored securely in password-protected systems
  • Clinical notes are kept separately from identifying details
  • I retain data only for as long as required by professional guidelines

Retention periods:

  • Adult clients: 7 years after the end of therapy
  • Young people: until age 25 (or 26 if seen at age 17)

After this period, records are securely destroyed.

5. Sharing your information

I will only share your information in the following situations:

  • If you give explicit consent
  • If there is a serious risk of harm to yourself or someone else
  • If required by law
  • For professional supervision — notes are discussed anonymously and without identifying details

I do not share information with insurers or employers unless you request it.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion (in some circumstances)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request transfer of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact me using the details at the top of this page.

If you have concerns about how your data has been handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at www.ico.org.uk.

7. Website and digital communication

  • My website uses standard cookies only for basic functionality
  • Emails, WhatsApp and other digital tools are not fully secure — please limit sensitive information when messaging

8. Updates

I occasionally update this privacy notice. The most recent version will always be available on this page.
